Barman V3 - Firmware Update

Connect Barman to your PC via USB and update the firmware directly from your browser (Chrome or Edge required).

⚠️ IMPORTANT:

Make sure the "Erase device" option is UNCHECKED before installing.

This ensures your Wi-Fi settings, total drinks statistics (life_total), and arm calibration remain intact.
Serial port driver required. If you encounter connection issues, try a different USB data cable.
